The historical verifier reported clip AUC of 0.96, an event-recall 95% interval of approximately 71–97%, and a false-alert interval of approximately 2–31 / hour. Different protocols and model-selection overlap prevent a controlled model comparison.

Candidate coverage increased from 74% to 92% across 50 labelled events (25 per dataset). Normal-footage candidate generation increased from 3,240 to 3,653 / hour. Coverage measures whether a candidate contains the labelled pair, rather than final detection accuracy.
